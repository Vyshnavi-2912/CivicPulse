Core Capabilities

Designed for Rural Villages & Urban Cities

CivicPulse bridges the gap between grassroots citizens and local authority departments with intelligent reporting tools.

AI-Assisted Detection

Upload a photograph and receive automated category suggestions (Pothole, Waste, Water Leakage) with confidence scores in demonstration mode.

Severity Assessment

Transparent rule-based priority engine evaluates physical safety risk, damage extent, and location type to assign Low, Medium, High, or Critical levels.

Location-Based Reporting

Supports explicit manual entry (State, District, Mandal, Village) and interactive Leaflet map pins so no rural corner is left unmapped.

Authority Information

Connect reported issues directly to local Gram Panchayat, R&B Road Departments, Municipality, and DISCOM Electricity official directory offices.

Sustainable Development Goal 11

Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable.

In many tier-2/3 towns and rural villages, infrastructure breakdown such as deep potholes, contaminated water leakage, and darkness at intersections remains unreported until severe accidents occur. CivicPulse empowers communities to make these invisible problems visible and actionable.
