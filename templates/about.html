Sustainable Cities & Communities

About CivicPulse

Building resilient civic technology to identify overlooked hazards in rural villages and urban cities.

UN SDG Goal 11 Alignment

Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable.

Target 11.3: Inclusive Urbanization

Empowers citizens in small rural villages and non-metro towns to report infrastructure defects without needing complex bureaucratic channels.

Target 11.6: Environmental Impact

Accelerates the detection of uncollected municipal waste dumps and drinking water pipeline bursts to prevent contamination and resource loss.

Target 11.7: Safe Public Spaces

Identifies dark streetlight junctions, dangerous potholes, and fallen trees to keep public thoroughfares safe for women, children, and commuters.

System Architecture & Design

Full-Stack Technology

  • Backend: Python 3 Flask web application framework
  • Database: Relational SQLite3 database (`civicpulse.db`)
  • Frontend: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 design system, JavaScript ES6
  • Mapping: Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap vector tiles
  • Analytics: Chart.js canvas graphing library

AI & Severity Engine

  • AI Vision Module: Modular classifier wrapper with live Demonstration Mode
  • Rule-Based Severity: Multi-factor calculation engine assessing safety risk, damage extent, and location priority
  • Authority Directory: Mapped local office database (Gram Panchayat, R&B, Water Board, DISCOM)
  • Admin Portal: Protected jury demonstration interface for status tracking
